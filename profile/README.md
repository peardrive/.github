PearDrive is an open-source effort to make peer-to-peer collaboration simple and practical. We build libraries and apps that let people share files, talk, and work together **directly between their devices**, with no central servers and no middleman holding their data.

Everything we make is **open source** and **non-commercial**. Every line of code is public, so anyone can read it, fork it, and build on it, and there are no paywalls, premium tiers, or hidden catches. We believe software that handles your files and conversations should be free to use and open to inspect, not a black box, and it will stay that way.

Finally, PearDrive is **open for contribution**. Bug fixes, new features, documentation, and ideas are all welcome. Open an issue or a pull request in any repository, or reach out to us directly.
